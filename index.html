How to Prepare Nigerian Egusi Soup

Introduction

Egusi Soup is one of Nigeria's most popular traditional dishes. It is prepared using ground melon seeds, vegetables, palm oil, assorted meat, fish, and a variety of seasonings. It is commonly enjoyed with swallow foods such as pounded yam, eba, fufu, or semovita.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time: 20 minutes

Cooking Time: 50 minutes

Servings: 4-6 people

Difficulty: Medium

Cuisine: Nigerian

Ingredients

You will need the following ingredients:

  • 2 cups of ground egusi (melon seeds)
  • 500g of assorted meat
  • 1 cup of palm oil
  • 2 cups of washed ugu (pumpkin leaves)
  • 1 medium-sized onion
  • 2 tablespoons of ground crayfish
  • 2 fresh tomatoes
  • 2 scotch bonnet peppers
  • 2 seasoning cubes
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 smoked fish
  • 1 stockfish
  • 2 cups of meat stock
  • Water as needed

Preparation Instructions

  1. Step 1: Prepare the Meat

    Wash the assorted meat thoroughly. Season with onions, salt, and seasoning cubes. Cook until tender and set aside.

  2. Step 2: Prepare the Egusi

    Mix the ground egusi with a little water to form a thick paste. Set aside.

  3. Step 3: Prepare the Pepper Mixture

    Blend the tomatoes, scotch bonnet peppers, and onions until smooth.

  4. Step 4: Heat the Palm Oil

    Place a pot over medium heat. Add palm oil and allow it to warm. Add chopped onions and stir.

  5. Step 5: Add the Egusi

    Add the egusi paste gradually into the pot. Stir and allow it to cook for a few minutes.

  6. Step 6: Add the Meat and Stock

    Add the cooked assorted meat, meat stock, smoked fish, stockfish, and ground crayfish. Stir gently.

  7. Step 7: Add Seasonings

    Add salt and seasoning cubes. Allow the soup to simmer so that all the ingredients combine properly.

  8. Step 8: Add Vegetables

    Wash and slice the ugu leaves. Add them to the soup and cook for another 5 minutes.

  9. Step 9: Serve

    Turn off the heat and allow the soup to cool slightly. Your delicious Egusi Soup is ready to serve!

Important Safety Tips

  • Hot oil: Be careful when handling heated palm oil to avoid burns.

  • Food hygiene: Always wash your hands, vegetables, meat, and cooking utensils before preparing food.

  • Cooking temperature: Ensure that meat and fish are properly cooked before adding them to the soup.

  • Knife safety: Handle kitchen knives carefully when cutting vegetables and other ingredients.

  • Warning: Avoid adding water to extremely hot oil because it can cause dangerous splashing.

Common Cooking Terms

Here are some cooking terms used in this recipe:

Simmering:
Cooking food gently at a temperature just below boiling point.
Blending:
Breaking down ingredients into a smooth mixture using a blender.
Seasoning:
Adding salt, spices, herbs, or other ingredients to improve the flavour of food.
Sautéing:
Cooking ingredients briefly in a small amount of hot oil.
Garnishing:
Adding finishing ingredients to improve the appearance or flavour of a dish.

Serving Suggestions

Egusi Soup can be served with:

  1. Pounded yam
  2. Eba (garri)
  3. Semovita
  4. Amala
  5. Fufu

Enjoy your delicious Nigerian Egusi Soup!
